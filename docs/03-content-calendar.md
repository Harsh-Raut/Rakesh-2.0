# PROJECT R — Content Plan
Content has one job: **manufacture proof** that a stranger can trust Project R with their event.

---

## The rule

Nobody books a DJ because of a clever caption. They book because they saw **a packed dancefloor** and thought "I want that at mine."

So: **every post shows either a full floor, the setup, or a happy client.** If a post shows none of those, it isn't marketing — it's a hobby.

## What to capture at every single event (non-negotiable)

| Shot | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| 3× wide crowd shots, floor packed | The proof. Most important frame you will ever take. |
| 1× vertical video of a peak moment (15–30s) | Reel fuel |
| 1× setup/rig shot before guests arrive | Sells the "full setup included" claim |
| 1× console close-up with lights on | Aesthetic filler, always useful |
| 1× entry or first-dance moment | Wedding clients buy this emotionally |
| 1× short video testimonial from the host | Worth more than 50 posts |

Fifteen minutes of phone footage per event, filed by date and event type, becomes a year of content. **Ask the wedding photographer for two crowd shots** — they're already there and usually happy to share.

## Weekly rhythm

| Day | Post | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | Crowd moment from the weekend | Proof, freshest |
| Tue | Setup / gear shot with a spec caption | Sells the package |
| Wed | Reel: song transition or a floor reaction | Reach |
| Thu | Client review screenshot or story repost | Trust |
| Fri | "Date open / booking [month]" + WhatsApp CTA | Direct conversion |
| Sat | Live stories from the night | Availability signal |
| Sun | Behind the scenes, or a planning-call tip | Personality |

## Ten Reel formats that work for DJs

1. **The floor at 9pm vs the floor at 12am.** Same frame, split screen. Proves the whole value proposition in six seconds.
2. **Crowd reaction to a specific drop.** Caption the song name — it's what people search for.
3. **Setup timelapse.** Empty hall → full rig. Sells professionalism silently.
4. **"Songs that never fail at an Indian wedding."** Listicle format, high saves, high shares.
5. **Entry-song moment.** Baraat or bride's entry, with the cue. Emotional, gets shared into family groups.
6. **"What ₹X gets you."** Show the actual rig for a package tier. Bold, filters bad leads, builds trust.
7. **Client testimonial, 20 seconds, filmed on the night** while they're happy.
8. **Request handled live** — someone asks, you mix it in, floor reacts.
9. **Common mistake explainer.** "Why your sangeet playlist doesn't work." Positions expertise.
10. **Dhol/live percussion moment.** Consistently the highest-engagement content in the Indian wedding market.

## Caption formula

`[What you're looking at] + [one useful or specific detail] + [booking CTA]`

> Reception, 600 guests, Jaipur. Held the entry track two minutes longer because the baraat was late — nobody noticed.
> **Booking Dec–Feb dates now. WhatsApp on the link.**

Every caption ends with a booking line. Every one.

## Seasonal push calendar

| Month | Focus |
|---|---|
| **Aug–Sep** | Hardest push of the year. Daily posting, "peak dates filling" urgency, ads live, planner meetings. |
| **Oct–Feb** | Post from events as they happen. Stories every night worked. Sell NYE by end of October. |
| **Mar–Apr** | College fests and corporate offsites — repurpose fest and party footage |
| **May–Jul** | Build mode: shoot a proper portfolio video, collect written testimonials, refresh the site, meet vendors |

## First 30 days (start here)

- **Days 1–3:** Set up Google Business Profile. Collect every photo and video that already exists, sorted by event.
- **Days 4–7:** Message past clients for reviews, individually. Target 10.
- **Days 8–14:** Publish the site with the real number, real photos, real prices. Update every bio link.
- **Days 15–21:** Post the best 6 pieces of existing footage. Establish the weekly rhythm.
- **Days 22–30:** Contact 20 wedding planners and 10 venues. Send them the site link and a one-page rate card.

That's the whole first month. It is unglamorous and it works better than anything else on this page.
